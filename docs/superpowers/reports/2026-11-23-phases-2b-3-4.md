## 1. What you can try now

### Phase 2b: the phone's record (app 0.4.0 work, shipped in 0.5.0)

- The phone can record calls, SMS, chat notifications and other notifications. Each kind has its own Collect switch,
  and each starts off.
- The record is folded so a message is stored once. If the PC is off, the phone keeps recording and sends it all, in
  order and once only, when the hub is back.
- The phone's Perms screen has an Allow button for each Android permission and a button for notification access.
  The dashboard shows a plain "what is missing" line on a tile when a permission is not given yet.
- The Record screen shows what is stored. Phone numbers and similar details are masked there.
- Stop Mini on the dashboard shows "Stopped from your PC" on the phone. Removing the phone in the dashboard
  shows "Your PC removed this phone." and stops the sync at once.
- Stop Mini on the phone does not end recording. It only stops Mini.

### Phase 3: Mini on the phone (app 0.5.0 and Mini Bot 0.8.0 work)

- A Brain screen with the setup steps for Termux and Claude Code, a Copy the line button, Check Termux, Update Mini
  Bot and Start Mini.
- A keeper that watches Mini's brain on the phone. The bot shows "asleep" if the brain is gone and starts it again.
  After 3 restarts in 10 minutes it stops trying until you start Mini.
- Two phone tools for Mini: `phone_status` and `phone_read`. Both obey the "Mini on this phone" list on the Perms
  screen. Each item can be on, ask or off, and starts off.
- Questions from Mini show on the phone with Yes and No. Yes works only from a real finger touch inside the app.
  The notification offers No and Open, never Yes.
- Voice: tap the bot, then Talk. It uses the phone's own offline speech. With no offline voice, replies show as text only.
- What Mini did on the phone appears in the dashboard's Activity after the next sync, with refusals.
- On the phone, Mini cannot use the web, run commands, or read files outside its work folder.

### Phase 4: Mini uses the hub, and the dashboard is yours

- On the PC, Mini gets four hub tools (`hub_read`, `hub_act`, `hub_log`, `hub_status`). They work only in Mini's own
  session. Every read and action shows in Activity.
- A mail account connector (IMAP and SMTP). You connect it with an app password. Mail shows in the Inbox and in a mail
  tile. Mini can read, search, draft, mark and archive. Sending and deleting always ask you first, with the whole text
  shown. One-time codes in mail are masked when Mini reads.
- Boards: edit mode, a tile gallery, several boards, and quick action buttons on tiles. Boards are kept across restarts.
- A command box with search over your calls, messages, notifications and mail, and "Go to" for Settings sections.
- Settings: how long to keep things (retention), delete by type, export a backup with a passphrase, import it back
  (with an undo), network details, look, start-up and boards.
